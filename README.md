# Kisan Setu

> Smart Agricultural Procurement & Logistics Platform

Kisan Setu is a smart, automation-first platform designed to make agricultural procurement more predictable, transparent, fair, and accessible for farmers.

## Problem

Farmers visiting procurement centres often face:

- Long and unpredictable waiting times
- No visibility into procurement capacity
- Weather-related crop damage risks
- Limited access for non-smartphone users
- Lack of transparency in weighing and grading
- Uncertainty about payment status
- Difficulty raising and tracking grievances

## Solution

Kisan Setu connects farmers, buyers, village agents, and mandi officials through a unified digital platform.

### Key Features

- Smart slot booking with real-time capacity locking
- Colour-coded procurement calendar
- Live queue and token tracking
- Weather-based travel risk alerts
- "Start Travelling Now" queue alerts
- Nearby procurement centre recommendations
- Offline QR-based gate check-in
- Transparent weighing and grading
- Photo proof at weighing
- Government-baseline pricing with controlled adjustments
- One-tap grievance management
- Payment status tracking
- Buyer anomaly detection
- Admin analytics dashboard
- Voice-based access for farmers
- SMS / IVR support
- Moving-average demand prediction
